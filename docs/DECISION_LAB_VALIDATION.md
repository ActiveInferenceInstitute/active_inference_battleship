## Scope and implementation

The extension adds a fourth workspace view, six diagnostic maps, selected-action reasoning with numeric GNN substitutions, two counterfactual posterior updates, β/γ sweeps, isolated controls, four illustrative presets, live/sandbox comparisons, factor inspection, and analysis export. Pure `src/core/analysis.ts` accepts catalogue/belief/public history only. Shared `InferenceControls`, `Board`, `ActionTable`, and token-based styling are composed into `DecisionMap`, `DecisionReceipt`, `PolicySensitivity`, `SandboxSettings`, and `FactorInspector`.

The GNN specification SHA remains `a425a3f6b0bdc8cb28a165f92c27c6ea329d3e48d2ea3f16136a93fbb447c91c`; its real upstream parser import and freshness check pass. No model horizon, likelihood semantics, game rules, selected-truth boundary, or replay format was broadened. `Belief.logWeights` exposes the already-retained log probabilities for stable incremental forecasts.
