## 1. Freeze the sampling and execution contract

- Board: 10 × 10 cells; ships [5,4,3,3,2], straight, nonoverlapping, touching permitted; 17 occupied cells. Use the unchanged catalog generator, which explicitly is **not** uniform over legal fleets (`fleet.ts:57–103`).
- Strata: N = 256 and 4096 labels; 128 newly generated catalog/truth cases in each, 256 independent paired units and 1,280 searches total. Each unit draws one fresh catalog and selects one label from it with a separate random stream. All five strategies receive that same catalog and selected truth but only their own public shot history. Do not reuse a catalog across cases or across sizes, use nested catalog prefixes, or count repeated runs, truths within a reused catalog, shots, or bootstrap draws as extra units.
- Sampling interpretation: independence across units is an assumption about separately derived pseudorandom streams, not a theorem implied by distinct seed integers. The target is the frozen generator/label-selection/random-policy distribution within each size. It is not unrestricted Battleship, a human population, competitive win rate, or an isolated catalog-size effect. Different sizes change both support and the truth pool. Do not pool strata or test a size interaction.
- Master seed: 20261025. Before any final search, materialize the entire case manifest. A concrete derivation is the first four SHA-256 digest bytes, read unsigned big-endian, of UTF-8 `aib-strategy-v1|20261025|final|N|i|role|j`, with decimal N, i = 0…127, role in `catalog,truth,random`, and collision counter j starting at 0. Allocate in size order 256,4096, then i, then role order above. Increment j only to reject a previously allocated 32-bit seed, a historical seed/offset stream state, or a recorded pilot stream seed. Record every accepted seed and counter. The source PRNG truncates seeds to 32 bits; checking untruncated integers is insufficient (`random.ts:9–12`). No performance-based rejection or seed replacement.
- Use the existing seeded index-selection routine for truth and random actions; preserve label multiplicity. Random has one stream per case, with no extra random-policy replicates counted as units. Pilots use a separate `pilot` namespace and are permanently excluded from final cases. Fix their list before allocating final seeds. Do not inspect final-case strategy outcomes during engineering.
- Freeze protocol JSON, case manifest JSON, exact source hashes including the new module, runtime versions, analysis/chart specification, and SHA-256 of exact JSON bytes in an append-only pre-execution manifest. Retain actual catalog hashes and selected label indices with traces. Hashes establish byte identity; retain a dated freeze receipt because hashes alone do not prove chronology. This note does not pretend those future artifacts already exist.

“No repeated truths as extra units” means no deliberate pseudoreplication. Independently generated catalogs can coincidentally select the same occupancy layout; log this without silently deduplicating or redrawing. The unit includes its fresh catalog. Requiring globally unique physical truths instead would change the sampling distribution and must be specified before execution, not introduced after inspection.

## 2. Fix strategies and selection

All profiles use truthful deterministic observations, assumed noise 0, no habits, and one-shot scores recomputed after each observation. Fix c = 2 and gamma = 4; gamma affects displayed softmax probabilities but not these greedy selectors.

| Profile | Maximized score on unvisited cells | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Random | Seeded uniform-index draw, ascending legal cell list | Ignores posterior for selection |
| Pragmatic | Predicted hit p | Greedy immediate hit forecast |
| Epistemic | Expected information gain I | Greedy label discrimination |
| Combined, beta = 1 | −G₁ | Standard one-step EFE specialization |
| Information-weighted, beta = 4 | −G₄ | Explicit weighted extension, not standard EFE |

At these settings, I = h(p), and G_beta = log(1+exp(2)) − 2p − beta h(p), using natural logarithms (`inference.ts:150–199`). Thus information is determined by hit probability here; the panels should explain the different objectives rather than imply independent information measurements or multi-step planning. Five profiles preserve the original combined setting while displaying a fixed weighting contrast. Keep all five in every chart/table even if traces or outcomes coincide.

For each nonrandom profile, compute the global maximum score m; retain scores ≥ m − 1e−12. Within that set compute the global maximum predicted hit pmax; retain p ≥ pmax − 1e−12. Choose the lowest cell index. Use inclusive absolute bands, not an approximate sort comparator, whose comparisons can be nontransitive. Reject nonfinite values. Random bypasses these bands.

## 3. Outcomes, traces, and failures

| Endpoint | Definition and denominator | Preferred direction |
|---|---|---|
| Primary T | Shot index at the 17th observed hit, integer 17…100 | Lower |
| Secondary K5 | Number of hits after exactly 5 shots, integer 0…5 | Higher |
| Secondary E5 | Posterior catalog-label entropy H5 / ln(N) | Lower for label discrimination only |

H is entropy over labels, including duplicate layouts; it is not entropy over distinct physical fleets or a task reward. With uniform labels and zero noise, Ht = ln(number of surviving labels), initially ln(N). Duplicate labels can leave positive terminal entropy even after all 17 cells are hit. Normalization makes initial values one; it does not make strata the same population.

Retain every actual legal shot from start through completion, with a hard cap of 100, including cells, outcomes, cumulative hits, predicted hit, EIG, posterior entropy, surviving-label count, controls, case/catalog/truth identities, random seed, source and runtime identities. “Full 100-shot legal traces” means **complete traces under a 100-shot cap**, not fabricated post-completion actions. Record time zero explicitly. Curves show t = 0…30; after completion carry hits = 17 and actual terminal entropy forward. Use all 128 units at every time, without conditioning on remaining unfinished cases. Completion distributions must still include all T through 100.

All units and profiles are mandatory. Repeated/out-of-bounds cells, impossible evidence, lost truth support, nonfinite diagnostics, missing profiles, or failure to complete by 100 abort the study; no exclusions, imputation, optional stopping, or replacement seeds. Preserve the failed attempt and logs. Any bug repair requires a documented protocol/source amendment and a complete consistent rerun on the frozen cases, never a silent mixture of implementations or seed search for significance.

## 4. Fixed analysis

For each non-pragmatic profile and each stratum, define raw paired differences d = profile − pragmatic separately for T, K5, and E5. Report mean(d) with its units and direction; negative favors the profile for T/E5, positive for K5. This pairing uses the shared catalog/truth case, not matching shots between policies. [NIST paired-observation documentation](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section3/prc311.htm) explains analysis through within-unit differences.

**Test.** Count n+ where d > epsilon, n− where d < −epsilon, and ties where |d| ≤ epsilon. Use epsilon = 0 for integer T/K5 and 1e−10 on normalized E5. Null: P(d > epsilon | |d| > epsilon) = 1/2. Conditional on m = n+ + n− independent, identically sampled non-tied units under that null, use `scipy.stats.binomtest(n_plus, m, p=0.5, alternative="two-sided")`. No symmetry or normality of difference magnitudes is required for this sign-probability null. This is not a test of equal means, full distributions, or generic equality of medians with mass at zero. If m = 0, set p = 1 by explicit protocol convention and mark “no non-tied cases”; do not call a binomial API with zero trials. See [SciPy binomtest](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.binomtest.html) and [statsmodels sign-test tie/empty-sample documentation](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.descriptivestats.sign_test.html).

**Multiplicity.** Exactly 4 profiles × 2 strata × 3 endpoints = 24 hypotheses, including all-tie p = 1 rows. Apply once: `multipletests(pvalues_24, alpha=0.05, method="holm")`. Freeze row order by size, profile order above excluding pragmatic, then T/K5/E5. Report raw and adjusted p and rejection for all 24. Shared baselines and endpoints induce dependence; Holm controls family-wise error with valid constituent p-values without requiring independent tests. [statsmodels correction API](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.multitest.multipletests.html); [official R documentation on Holm dependence validity](https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-patched/library/stats/html/p.adjust.html). T is the primary endpoint, but receives no separate alpha allocation. No between-size, between-nonbaseline-profile, per-timepoint, subgroup, or trace-level inferential additions.

**Magnitude and uncertainty.** Within each stratum bootstrap 128 case indices with replacement, 10,000 draws; preserve all five profiles, endpoints, and curve times together for each sampled index. Freeze PCG64 seed 20261026 for N = 256 and 20261027 for N = 4096, runtime/library versions, and the resampled index-matrix hash. Calculate mean raw d in each draw, taking 2.5th/97.5th percentiles with linear interpolation. An equivalent single-contrast call uses `bootstrap((profile, pragmatic), mean_difference, paired=True, n_resamples=10000, method="percentile", confidence_level=0.95, rng=...)`; SciPy defaults to BCa, so specify percentile explicitly. [SciPy bootstrap documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.bootstrap.html).

These are approximate, descriptive 95% intervals for mean effects, not simultaneous intervals and not inversion of the sign tests. Percentile intervals have no guaranteed finite-sample coverage; do not read zero exclusion as the Holm decision. Retain ties and tiny raw entropy differences in means/bootstrap, without rounding them to zero. Exactly all-zero differences yield [0,0]; epsilon-classified all-ties need not. Neither establishes equivalence or general equality. Report profile wins/ties/losses as counts and fractions of **128**; optionally show conditional wins/(wins+losses) with its explicit denominator and “undefined” if zero. Here “win” means a favorable endpoint difference, never a competitive game win.

No test change is necessary for this estimand. If mean completion improvement is the intended hypothesis, the sign test does not answer it: a paired mean test would have to replace the test contract before final data. Do not automatically switch to Wilcoxon: its symmetry null and zero/tie handling differ ([SciPy Wilcoxon](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.wilcoxon.html)). Keep the present narrower interpretation for simplicity.

## 5. Computation gate before production

Implement a **new** study module: uniform posterior on surviving original labels, filtering by public observations at zero noise. Preserve duplicate labels; never collapse physical layouts or inspect selected truth in inference/selection. Reuse unchanged `scoreActions` and source controls; use unchanged full-history `infer` as the numerical oracle. Apply the **new robust selector to both implementations** for exact choice comparison; requiring agreement with the historical exact-sort selector would test a different rule.

On predetermined pilot/fixture histories, compare every cell's occupancy, posterior entropy, and every legal action's EIG to the oracle with absolute error ≤ 1e−12; also compare G, predicted hit, support, and exact selected cells at every step for all profiles. Random must consume identical streams. Exact choice agreement is an independent gate: a 1e−12 numerical bound alone does not ensure a 1e−12 tie-band choice. Exercise threshold boundaries, shuffled action arrays, duplicate labels, guaranteed hits/misses, asymmetric support, and complete trajectories. Use bounded pilot cases at both target sizes, not final seeds. Preserve source hashes and all discrepancies. Abort production on any mismatch; never widen tolerances to obtain a pass.

Before trusting validators, require deliberate failures: perturb an occupancy/EIG value by 2e−12; choose an illegal/repeated cell; drop a paired row; change a catalog hash; supply impossible evidence. The corresponding checks must reject them. Analysis fixtures: all zero → p = 1 and CI [0,0]; 10 positive/0 negative → raw p = 1/512; adding 118 ties leaves that p unchanged while restoring denominator 128; paired reordering preserves results. With those 10 non-ties in one row and all other 23 raw p = 1, Holm adjusted p is 24/512 = 0.046875. Distinguish this arithmetic fixture from study data. Independent implementation review remains a later gate; this research note is not an audit of a new implementation.
